⚙️ Kaise kaam karta hai?

  • TXT mein original line breaks/blank lines rakhta hai. PDF mein page-wise text extract karta hai aur two-column pages par left-to-right reading order ka andaza lagata hai.
  • Q1., Q2., Ex:, 😂, ✅, (A)/(B)/(a)/(b), standalone numbers aur symbols ko protect karne ki koshish karta hai.
  • Multiple lines ko ek request mein batch karta hai aur kuch batches parallel chalata hai, taaki translation tez ho.
  • PDF export translated text se ek nayi, printable PDF banata hai. Yeh original PDF ke images, diagrams, exact fonts ya page layout ko bilkul same nahi rakhta. Scanned/image-only PDF ke liye OCR chahiye, jo is version mein nahi hai.
  • Translation Google Translate ke public, unofficial web endpoint se hota hai. Yeh free hai, lekin Google ki service badalne ya rate limit karne par fail ho sakta hai.

Important: koi bhi automatic translator 100% perfect matra/grammar ki guarantee nahi de sakta. Exam content mein names, facts, legal terms aur answer keys ko ek baar review zaroor karein.
